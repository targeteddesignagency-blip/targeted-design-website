## The Golden Circle

Simon Sinek's framework: leaders who inspire think, act, and communicate from the inside out.

```
     ╭─────────────╮
    ╱     WHY       ╲        ← Purpose, cause, belief
   ╱    (Why exist?)  ╲
  │                   │
  │    ╭───────────╮  │
  │   ╱    HOW      ╲ │       ← Process, actions at natural best
  │  ╱  (How we do it)╲│
  │  │               │ │
  │  │   ╭───────╮   │ │
  │  │  │  WHAT  │   │ │    ← Products, services, results
  │  │  │(What we│   │ │
  │  │  │  do)   │   │ │
  ╰──╰──╰───────╯───╯─╯
```

**Most companies communicate outside-in:** WHAT → HOW → WHY
**Inspiring companies communicate inside-out:** WHY → HOW → WHAT

**"People don't buy WHAT you do, they buy WHY you do it."**

## Why Inside-Out Matters

**Outside-in (WHAT → HOW → WHY):**
"We do EDDM marketing with AI tools because it's profitable."
Result: People understand what you do, but don't feel inspired.

"We exist to protect local businesses from market erosion. We use AI-first marketing to plant windbreaks. The result is EDDM campaigns that build resilient client forests."
Result: People believe what you believe. They join the cause.
